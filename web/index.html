visor

drag to turn · scroll to zoom. Left half of the canvas is one side of the face, right half the other, and they meet at the nose - so the middle columns are the bridge.

Every value is RGBA. A single number is that number in R, G and B with alpha 1, and a node that wants one number reads R. Unconnected inputs fall back to the node's own widget. The LED Output flattens alpha against black - the panel has nothing behind it.

The preview runs the compiled program, not the nodes: download .bin ships exactly this, images included, and the head's upload page takes it from there.
